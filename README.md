### 🔭 Recent Research

**Multi-Component Aerosol & Micellization Modeling → (ATMOS Collaborative Research | 2026)**  
Extended thermodynamic Köhler and Monolayer frameworks in Python to simulate realistic mixed organic–inorganic–insoluble aerosol particles (SDS-NaCl-insoluble cores) modeled after experimental systems investigated by Dr. Ana Kroflič. Evaluated the coupled effects of dry particle composition, micelle aggregation numbers, counterion binding, and Pitzer-Debye-Hückel ionic activities (CISA) on bulk-surface partitioning, surface tension, water activity, and cloud condensation nuclei (CCN) activation across sub-micron droplet growth spectra.  
*Tools: Python, SciPy, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, LaTeX*

**Atmospheric Computational Modeling → Master's Thesis (ATMOS | Oct 2025 – Apr 2026)**  
Developed, ported, and validated a Python-based thermodynamic modeling suite (`Mono`, `MonoMic`, `CluMic`, `CluMicNa`, and `CISA`) to investigate bulk-surface partitioning and the surface activity of atmospheric surfactants in aerosol nanodroplets. Combined constrained numerical root-finding with statistical and sensitivity analyses to evaluate how monolayer depletion, pre-micellar clustering, and micellization above the Critical Micelle Concentration (CMC) govern hygroscopic growth and droplet size effects.  
*Tools: Python, Numerical Optimization, Thermodynamic Modeling, Statistical Analysis*

**LLM-Driven Behavioral Profiling → (UBICOMP | Jun 2025 – Sep 2025)**  
Built an intelligent digital platform integrating generative AI and machine learning for behavioral analysis and risk classification. Developed robust backend services to deliver real-time feedback, integrating LLM-based insights (Mistral-7B) to create a functional, practical prototype.

### 🌍 Recent Projects

**Pocket Geologist → Soil Erodibility Prediction from Images (Nov 2026)**  
Built a computer vision system that predicts how easily flowing water erodes soil, directly from smartphone photographs. Benchmarked 4 CNN backbones (ConvNeXt-Tiny, EfficientNet-B0, MobileNetV3-Large, ResNet-50) with 3 classifier heads (Dual-Pool MLP, Cosine, MLP), achieving 93.50% test accuracy on 9 soil classes. The system maps predictions to USDA K-factors and outputs a 1–10 erodibility risk score with probability-weighted confidence.  
*Tools: PyTorch, torchvision, Transfer Learning, TTA, Grad-CAM, scikit-learn*

**Rice Disease Detection → Multi-Backbone CNN Comparison (2026)**  
Trained and compared 10 pretrained CNN architectures (ConvNeXt-Base, DenseNet-121, EfficientNetV2-S, InceptionResNetV2, InceptionV3, MobileNetV2, MobileNetV3-Large, ResNet-50, ResNet-50V2, VGG-16, Xception) for rice leaf disease classification. Extended the pipeline with autoencoder-based feature decoding, reverse image search, and hybrid ML classifiers for interpretable diagnosis.  
*Tools: TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV, scikit-learn, Autoencoders, CBIR*

**Face Recognition Pipeline → (CSE422 | 2026)**  
Developed an end-to-end face recognition system using InceptionResNetV2 deep feature extraction (1536-D) combined with wavelet transforms and data augmentation. Compared 7 classifiers (SVM Linear/RBF/Sigmoid, Random Forest, Naive Bayes, MLP, Ensemble Voting), achieving 94% test accuracy with the ensemble approach on multi-person group photo recognition.  
*Tools: TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV, PyWavelets, scikit-learn*

**ML-Based Agricultural Monitoring → (BSc Thesis | Nov 2022 – Dec 2023)**  
Developed a CNN-based image recognition system for agricultural disease classification using image preprocessing and augmentation. Extracted deep visual features and integrated machine learning classifiers to develop a robust computational approach for environmental monitoring.
